Description

This food recipe is my first successful business. But it needs lot of effort to make the dough alone and deep frying it.

This recipe is a good snack for afternoon.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp butter/margarine or 1 cube per 1 cup water
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 liter oil

Preparation

    Step

  1. Combine the water, sugar salt, and butter in a pan.

  2. Heat the pan and bring it to simmer
  3. Make sure the butter is melted
  4. Turn off the heat, then add the flour and mix and knead until it becomes smooth dough
  5. Let the dough cool in a dry place
  6. Add the egg and mix it until smooth and has sticky texture
  7. put the finish dough in the piping bag then deep fry it
