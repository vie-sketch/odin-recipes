Pizza Roll

Home

Description

I was told by my mother-in-law about this dish. She told me to use this recipe to sell in my workplace. My fiance also nodded and supported the idea.

This recipe is inspired to be a quick snack either for breakfast or for lunch.

Ingredients

  • 1 Loaf Bread
  • 5 Chicken Hotdog
  • 2 Pizza Sauce small pack
  • Cheese bar
  • Bread Crumbs 500 grams
  • 4 Medium Eggs

Preparation

  • Prepare Rolling pin
  • Plates or bowls
  • Beat the eggs in one bowl

Steps

  1. Flatten the loaf Bread using a rolling pin or any object that can flat the bread
  2. Put enough amount pizza sauce and spread it over the face of the bread
  3. After putting the pizza sauce, slice the chicken hotdog into four and put one in the loaf bread with pizza sauce
  4. Slice the cheese also into rectangle and put two piece of it to the load bread with sauce together with the hotdog slice
  5. Roll the loaf bread carefully and tightly. Make sure the hotdog and cheese slices won't fall of in the sides
  6. After rolling, dip it in beaten eggs in the bowl
  7. Then cover it with bread crumbs
